Practice 01

Legal Tech

Systems for law firms, in-house legal departments and government legal affairs — built around the matter, not around a generic ticket queue.

Arabic-first document handling, Hijri and Gregorian date support, and workflows that reflect how litigation and advisory work actually move through a Saudi practice.

  • Matter and case management with deadlines, hearings and limitation tracking
  • Contract lifecycle management: templates, clause libraries, approvals and e-signature
  • Document assembly and Arabic legal drafting assistance
  • Court and e-government integration paths, including Najiz filings and Nafath identity
  • Legal research and precedent search over your own knowledge base
  • Billing, time capture, trust accounting and client reporting
  • Legal spend analytics and matter dashboards for general counsel
Practice 02

Medico-Legal

Where a clinical record becomes evidence. We build the systems that carry medical findings safely into legal and insurance processes.

Structured reporting, controlled access, and an audit trail that survives scrutiny — for insurers, expert witnesses and legal counsel alike.

  • Medico-legal case files linking clinical records, findings and legal matters
  • Structured medical report templates with review and sign-off workflows
  • Injury, disability and impairment assessment workflows
  • Expert-witness scheduling, instruction packs and opinion tracking
  • Evidence handling with chain-of-custody logging and tamper-evident audit trails
  • Insurance medical claim review and adjudication support
  • Integration with hospital, clinic and laboratory systems (HL7 / FHIR where available)

Common questions

Before you brief us

Do you replace our existing systems or work with them?

Both are possible, and we will say which we think is right. In most engagements the fastest value comes from connecting what you already run — practice management, ERP, clinical systems — and building only the missing layer.

Can the platform be hosted inside the Kingdom?

Yes. We design for in-Kingdom hosting where data classification or regulator guidance requires it, and can advise on the residency implications of each option under PDPL and NCA cloud controls.

Is the software available in Arabic?

Arabic is a first-class language in everything we build — interface, documents, search and reporting — with English available alongside it. Right-to-left layout is designed, not machine-translated.

How long does a first delivery take?

A focused first release typically runs 8–16 weeks depending on integration scope. Discovery is short and paid separately so you can stop after it if the business case does not hold.

Do you work outside Saudi Arabia?

Yes. We deliver in the UAE, and support regional clients through our partner network — NextEdge Technologies in Dubai, and NavyBits across the wider region.

Which practice do you need first?

Send us the problem in a paragraph. We will tell you which of our practices should lead, and what a first phase would look like.
