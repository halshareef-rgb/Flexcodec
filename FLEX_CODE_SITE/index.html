Why Flex Code

Built for regulated work in the Kingdom

Legal and medico-legal data is among the most sensitive a Saudi organisation holds. We design for that reality first, then make the system pleasant to use.

  • Arabic-first interfaces and documents, with full bilingual English support
  • Aligned to the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) and SDAIA guidance
  • Architected against the National Cybersecurity Authority Essential Cybersecurity Controls
  • In-Kingdom hosting options and clear data-residency choices
  • Integration paths to national platforms such as Najiz, Nafath and ZATCA e-invoicing
