Contract Lifecycle

Templates and clause libraries, guided drafting, negotiation history, approval routing, e-signature and an obligations register with renewal alerts.

Medico-Legal Case Platform

Arabic Legal AI Assistant

Legal Operations Analytics

Deployment

Your data, your choice of ground

Deployment is a compliance decision before it is a technical one. We help you make it deliberately.

  • In-Kingdom cloud hosting for regulated and classified workloads
  • Private or on-premises deployment where policy requires it
  • Regional cloud for GCC operations
  • Documented data-residency, retention and deletion positions
  • Role-based access, segregation of duties and full audit logging
  • Encryption in transit and at rest, with key-management options
